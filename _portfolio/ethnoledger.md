**Published at DeSci Lagos 2026 (Lead Author)**

EthnoLedger is a USSD-blockchain hybrid system for documenting and preserving indigenous knowledge in communities with limited smartphone or internet penetration.

Knowledge holders contribute via USSD (basic phone, no data required). Contributions are hashed, timestamped, and recorded on-chain — creating a tamper-resistant archive with cryptographic provenance. A bridge service translates USSD session data into chain transactions without requiring contributors to know anything about blockchain.

Designed for West African deployment contexts where indigenous ecological and agricultural knowledge is at risk of loss and where conventional app-based platforms cannot reach the knowledge holders themselves.
