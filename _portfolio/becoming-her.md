**Ongoing — IDENA Brand**

*Becoming Her* is a webinar and workshop series I founded and run under my IDENA brand, focused on women building technical careers in engineering in Nigeria. The format is live sessions with real practitioners — not inspirational talks, but practical ones about internships, research, hardware, and navigating male-majority engineering departments.

**Becoming Her 2.0 (June 2026):** 90+ live attendees from universities across Nigeria and the diaspora. I built the registration web app and raffle tool, and managed email campaigns to 64+ registrants. Sessions covered backend and hardware engineering paths, research portfolio building, and honest conversations about what it's like to be a woman in Nigerian engineering.

**Community reach:** 300+ followers across the IDENA platform. Attendees from UNILAG, LAUTECH, FUTA, OAU, and others.

**Edition 3** is in planning. The format will expand to include structured workshops and guest engineers with more diverse backgrounds.

The series exists because I looked for it and it wasn't there.
