**Published in EEG 213: Signals and Systems, University of Lagos, 2026**

A Python-based DSP framework that captures generator acoustic signals, extracts 9 time- and frequency-domain features via FFT, and predicts remaining useful life (RUL) using linear regression.

**Why this matters in Nigeria:** Load shedding means residential generators run near-continuously in urban areas. Most households have no maintenance signal until failure. This system turns acoustic data — already available from a phone microphone — into an actionable estimate of how much life the generator has left.

**Validated on MIMII industrial machine recordings** (standard benchmark dataset), then packaged as a multilingual web application in English, Igbo, Yoruba, and Hausa — the four most widely spoken languages in Nigeria — to make the tool accessible without English fluency.
