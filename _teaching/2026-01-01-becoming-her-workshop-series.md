Organiser and host of *Becoming Her*, an ongoing webinar and workshop series under the IDENA brand for women pursuing engineering careers in Nigeria. Sessions cover technical skills (backend development, hardware fundamentals, research methods), career navigation (internship applications, CV writing, interview prep), and community — with guest speakers from across the Nigerian tech and engineering industry.

The series draws attendees from universities across Nigeria and the Nigerian diaspora, and has grown a community of over 300 followers.
