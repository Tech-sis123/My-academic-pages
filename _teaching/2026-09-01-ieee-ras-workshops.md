As chair of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society student chapter at UNILAG, I organise and facilitate hands-on technical workshops for engineering students. Topics have included:

- **FPGA fundamentals** — HDL design flow, synthesis and simulation in Vivado
- **ROS basics** — Robot Operating System architecture, nodes, topics and services
- **Embedded systems for autonomous agents** — sensor interfacing, motor control, real-time constraints on microcontrollers and FPGAs

Sessions run monthly and are open to all UNILAG engineering students. The chapter also competes in regional robotics challenges.
