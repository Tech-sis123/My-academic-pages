Founder of **Unilag Female Engineers '29**, a community I started for female Computer and Electrical/Electronics Engineering students in the Class of 2029 at UNILAG. The community runs peer study sessions, technical workshops (Python, circuit design, embedded systems), and mentoring circles connecting junior members with industry contacts.

Focus areas include closing the confidence gap in technical interviews, building project portfolios early, and navigating the male-majority environment of engineering departments in Nigerian universities.
