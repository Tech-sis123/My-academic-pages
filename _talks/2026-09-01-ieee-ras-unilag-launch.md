Delivered the founding address at the inaugural meeting of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society student chapter at the University of Lagos, which I chair. The talk outlined the chapter's mission to build a student-led robotics and autonomous systems community at UNILAG, mapped existing robotics research opportunities across the university, and set out a programme of workshops, competitions, and industry visits for the year.

The chapter has since run sessions on FPGA-based hardware acceleration, robot operating system (ROS) basics, and embedded systems for autonomous agents.
