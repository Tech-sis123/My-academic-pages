Served as an invited judge at the **ELI SHE-DEBATES Secondary School Debate Competition 2026**, organised by the Engineering Ladies Initiative (ELI). The event was held on 29th July 2026 at the Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE) Hall at the University of Lagos.

SHE-DEBATES is a competition for secondary school students designed to build public speaking, critical reasoning, and confidence in young women interested in science, technology, and engineering careers. As a judge, I evaluated contestants on argumentation, evidence, delivery, and rebuttals, and delivered verbal feedback to participants after rounds.
