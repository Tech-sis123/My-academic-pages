Presented the Fair Mining Deal framework at the Russian-African Forum of Young Scientists, co-hosted by Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. The talk introduced the Fair Mining Deal Index (FMDI) — a bilateral pre-signing tool for assessing natural resource agreements between Russian companies and African states — and walked through its retrospective validation on the 2005 Liberia–Mittal Steel agreement, which correctly predicted the renegotiation within one year.

Co-presented with Sofia A. Afanasieva and Polina S. Antonyuk (SPbPU).
