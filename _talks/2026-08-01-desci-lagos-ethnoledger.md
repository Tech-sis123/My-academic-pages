Presented **EthnoLedger** as lead author at DeSci Lagos 2025 — a decentralised science event focused on applying Web3 and open research infrastructure to African challenges.

EthnoLedger is a USSD-blockchain hybrid system for documenting and preserving indigenous knowledge in communities with limited smartphone or internet penetration. The architecture allows community members to contribute knowledge via USSD (basic phone keypad, no data required), with each contribution cryptographically hashed and recorded on-chain, creating a tamper-resistant, community-owned archive.

The project addresses the dual risks of indigenous knowledge loss — cultural erosion and the absence of immutable provenance — and is designed for deployment in contexts across West Africa where digital literacy and connectivity are uneven.
