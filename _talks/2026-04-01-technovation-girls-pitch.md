Selected as an **Africa Regional Honoree** at the Technovation Girls Global Hackathon 2026 — one of the world's largest technology education programs for young women, run by Technovation.

The regional honoree distinction is awarded to teams whose apps and pitches are judged among the strongest in the Africa region before the global finals. The competition evaluates a working mobile application, a business plan, a pitch video, and a pitch presentation.

Technovation Girls is a UN-recognised program focused on building the next generation of women tech innovators across emerging markets.
