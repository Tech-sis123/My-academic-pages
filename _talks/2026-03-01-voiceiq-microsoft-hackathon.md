Led the idea conception and pitch for **VoiceIQ** at the Microsoft AI Skills Hackathon 2026 — finishing **1st place** nationally.

VoiceIQ is a real-time telecom conversation intelligence platform built for Nigerian English and Pidgin code-switching. Core components include:

- **NLP for code-switching** — language identification and sentiment analysis across mixed English/Pidgin utterances
- **XGBoost + SHAP churn modelling** — predicts subscriber churn risk mid-conversation with explainability
- **Agent Action Cards** — live in-call prompts that surface retention offers, escalation triggers, and next-best-action recommendations to call-centre agents

The project was designed for Nigerian telecom operators facing high subscriber churn in competitive, price-sensitive markets where existing English-only NLP tools miss crucial Pidgin signals.
