Hosted a session in the *Becoming Her* webinar series run under the IDENA brand, aimed at women building engineering careers in Nigeria. The session covered practical pathways into backend engineering, hardware, and research; how to navigate internship applications and technical interviews; and how to build a research portfolio early in an undergraduate degree. Q&A drew attendees from universities across Nigeria and the diaspora.

*Becoming Her* is an ongoing series focused on representation and practical career development for women in STEM.
