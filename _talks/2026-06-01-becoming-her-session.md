Hosted **Becoming Her 2.0**, the second edition of the *Becoming Her* webinar series run under my IDENA brand — a platform for women building engineering careers in Nigeria. The session drew **90+ live attendees** from universities across Nigeria and the diaspora.

Topics covered practical pathways into backend engineering, hardware, and research; how to navigate internship applications and technical interviews; and how to build a research portfolio early in an undergraduate degree. I also built the registration web app and raffle tool for the event, and managed BCC email campaigns to 64+ registrants.

*Becoming Her* is now planning its 3rd edition. The series focuses on representation, practical career development, and community for women in STEM.
